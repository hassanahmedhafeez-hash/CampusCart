A little about us

Built around student life.

CampusCart makes everyday campus essentials easier to discover.

Our idea

Student life has enough to think about.

Between lectures, assignments, group projects and everything else, finding useful everyday items should feel simple. CampusCart is a student-focused demo store that brings practical essentials together in one easy-to-browse place.

This project demonstrates a multi-page e-commerce experience using HTML, CSS and JavaScript — including product browsing, cart management and a simulated checkout.
